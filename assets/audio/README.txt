Last Show audio

chapter_one/ contains the supplied sound pack, indexed by manifest.json.
Its SOURCE.txt documents the sound sources, cue sheet and licensing information.
Ambience and music use Ogg Vorbis; short effects and character sounds use WAV.

full_game/ contains additional cues for the roof and home, contract signing,
item pickups, rope cutting and the jump. See full_game/SOURCE.txt.

Playback and volume buses are handled by scripts/chapter_audio.gd.
